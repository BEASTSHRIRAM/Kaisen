THE 5 DEFENSES EXPLAINED BRIEFLY
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Defense 1: INPUT VALIDATION
  What:     Catch extreme values
  Example:  CPU = -50% → ❌ IMPOSSIBLE
  Works on: Obvious attacks
  Cost:     Very low
  
Defense 2: METRIC CONSISTENCY
  What:     Ensure metrics make sense together
  Example:  CPU 5% + Connections 500 → ❌ IMPOSSIBLE
  Works on: Illogical patterns
  Cost:     Very low
  
Defense 3: CONFIDENCE THRESHOLD
  What:     Only trust confident decisions
  Example:  Q-values [5.0, 4.99] → Too close → ⚠️ ESCALATE
  Works on: Borderline cases
  Cost:     Very low
  
Defense 4: ENSEMBLE VOTING
  What:     Use 3 models, need agreement
  Example:  2/3 models agree → Not enough → ⚠️ ESCALATE
  Works on: Model-specific attacks
  Cost:     Medium (3x GPU memory)
  
Defense 5: ADVERSARIAL TRAINING
  What:     Train on adversarial examples
  Example:  Model learns to spot fake metrics
  Works on: All attack types
  Cost:     Medium (1-2 hours retraining)

METRIC CHEAT SHEET
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ASR (Attack Success Rate):
  • 0-10%: Excellent (robust)
  • 10-20%: Good (acceptable)
  • 20-50%: Moderate (concerning)
  • >50%: Poor (very vulnerable)

Robustness Score:
  • 0-50%: Low (needs work)
  • 50-80%: Moderate (acceptable)
  • 80-90%: High (good)
  • 90-100%: Excellent (production-ready)

Confidence:
  • High (>0.75): Trust the decision
  • Medium (0.5-0.75): Consider escalating
  • Low (<0.5): Always escalate

Ensemble Agreement:
  • >85%: Trust the vote
  • 70-85%: Acceptable
  • <70%: Escalate

COMMON QUESTIONS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Why do I need 5 defenses?
A: Defense in depth. Each catches different attacks.
   Think: lock + alarm + camera + dog + insurance

Q: Will it make the system slower?
A: +7ms total. Imperceptible to users. Worth it for security.

Q: Can an attacker bypass all 5?
A: Theoretically yes, but extremely hard in practice.
   Would need to fool 3+ models + pass 5 independent checks.

Q: How often do I retrain?
A: Weekly for best results. Can do daily if new attacks found.

Q: What if I only use 2-3 defenses?
A: Better than nothing, but 5 is needed for 99%+ robustness.

Q: Do I need GPU for all 5?
A: Yes, ensemble needs GPU. CPU alone too slow.
