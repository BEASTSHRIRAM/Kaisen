EXPECTED REVIEW COMMENTS & RESPONSES
================================================================================

Q: "This is all synthetic data. How does it work on real infrastructure?"
A: Acknowledged in Limitations (Section 7). Future work includes real 
   deployment. Synthetic data allows controlled evaluation of core hypothesis.

Q: "Max-fusion baseline is weak. Why not try weighted ensemble?"
A: Max-fusion is the true baseline for "no coordination." Learned arbitration
   is our contribution. We could compare to learned fusion (future work).

Q: "What about adversarial robustness of the RL agent?"
A: Excellent point. Acknowledged in Limitations. This is future work (Section 7).
   Current paper focuses on detection accuracy under normal circumstances.

Q: "Why not use more sophisticated RL algorithms (PPO, A3C, etc.)?"
A: DQN is standard for IDS applications (per literature survey). More complex
   algorithms could be explored, but DQN suffices for this proof-of-concept.

Q: "How does performance scale to thousands of agents/hosts?"
A: Scalability is future work. Current evaluation is single-agent. Section 7
   discusses deployment across multi-tenant environments.
